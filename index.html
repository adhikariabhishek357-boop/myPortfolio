About Me

A life guided by
compassion & purpose

As a Disability Support Facilitator (DSF), I walk alongside individuals living with disabilities, helping them navigate life with dignity, confidence, and independence. Every person deserves support that sees their whole self.

Currently in my fourth year of Management studies at Panchtar Multiple Campus, I blend academic knowledge in organizational thinking with hands-on human-centered work. I believe that effective support comes from both the heart and the mind.

Born and raised in Nepal, I carry with me the warmth of a close-knit family — values passed down through generations that remind me every day why this work matters.
