About Me

Hello! I'm Abhishek Adhikari, a dedicated and compassionate professional serving as a Disability Support Facilitator (DSF) at Hilihag Rural Municipality, Panchami. I am committed to empowering individuals with disabilities by connecting them with the resources, services, and support they need to live with dignity and independence. My work focuses on building inclusive communities where every person — regardless of ability — has equal access to opportunities and respect.
